About CapLink

Why CapLink?

Money decisions shouldn't feel like guesswork. CapLink is designed to make financial concepts easier to understand and help people make more informed financial decisions.

Understand before you decide.

Renting or buying, paying cash or taking a loan, investing or prepaying — the right answer depends on your numbers. Yet most people make these calls with rough guesses or one-size-fits-all advice.

CapLink puts comparison tools, calculators, a Financial Health check, and an AI assistant in one friendly app. You enter the information that matters to your situation, and CapLink helps you see the trade-offs clearly — so the final decision is an informed one, and it stays yours.

What we focus on

Clarity

Clean screens, plain language, and results you can actually read.

Education

Explanations that help you learn the "why" behind every number.

Comparison

Side-by-side views that make trade-offs visible before you commit.

Planning

Tools to think ahead — for purchases, goals, and retirement.

Awareness

A clearer view of your financial health, so nothing catches you off guard.

Privacy

No bank linking. You decide what information to enter.

What CapLink is — and isn't

CapLink is a financial education, calculation, comparison, and decision-support app. To be clear about its role, CapLink is not:

  • A bank or financial institution
  • A broker or investment adviser
  • A lender or insurance provider
  • A tax authority or tax filing service
  • A source of guaranteed returns or savings
  • A replacement for professional advice
