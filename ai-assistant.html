What does my EMI result mean?

Your EMI is the fixed amount you'd repay each month. In the early years, more of it goes toward interest; later, more goes toward the principal. Choosing a shorter tenure usually raises the EMI but can reduce the total interest you pay.Illustrative example — not a real conversation.

Should I pick a shorter tenure?

It depends on how comfortably the higher EMI fits your monthly budget and emergency fund. You could compare both tenures in the Loan calculator to see the difference.

How CapLink Assistant helps

Explains concepts

Plain-language explanations of SIPs, EMIs, emergency funds, inflation, and more — no jargon required.

Interprets results

Helps you understand what your calculator outputs and Financial Health breakdown might mean for you.

Suggests next steps

Using the Assistant wisely

  • Treat responses as a starting point for your own research, not a final answer.
  • Double-check figures, especially for tax, insurance, or legal matters.
  • Share only what's needed for your question. Never enter bank passwords, OTPs, card numbers, CVV, or UPI PINs — CapLink will never ask for them.
  • For important or complex decisions, consult an appropriately qualified professional.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is CapLink Assistant?

CapLink Assistant is an AI chat feature inside the CapLink app. It answers financial questions in plain language, explains calculator results, and suggests relevant CapLink tools, using information you choose to share.

What can I ask CapLink Assistant?

You can ask about topics such as reducing debt, retirement readiness, saving, financial goals, major purchases, general financial concepts, or what a calculator result means.

Can AI replace a financial advisor?

No. CapLink Assistant provides educational information for awareness and planning. It does not know every detail of your situation and is not a substitute for personalised advice from a qualified professional.

Why should I verify AI-generated financial information?

AI responses are generated automatically and can be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect. Double-check important figures and, for tax, investment, insurance, loan, or legal decisions, confirm with official sources or a qualified professional.
