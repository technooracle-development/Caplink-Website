How to delete your account

Account deletion is available inside the CapLink app. It takes four steps.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Open CapLink

    Open the CapLink mobile application and sign in to your account.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Open your Profile

    Go to the Profile section of the CapLink app.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Open Account Settings

    In your Profile, open the account/settings section, then select Delete Account.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Confirm deletion

    Follow the confirmation steps shown in the app to confirm that you want to delete your account.

Menu labels may vary slightly between app versions. If you can't find the option, see Can't access your account? below.

What happens when you delete your account?

Deleting your CapLink account starts the account deletion process for information associated with your account, subject to applicable legal, security, and operational requirements.

Depending on the data stored by the service, account-associated information may include:

Account information

Information associated with your CapLink account, such as your account/profile information.

Financial profile

Financial information you manually entered into CapLink for calculations, comparisons, Financial Health features, and personalized insights.

Saved calculations

Calculations and saved information associated with your account, where applicable.

User activity

Account-related activity information associated with your CapLink account, where applicable.

AI insights / chat data

AI-generated insights or conversation-related information associated with your account, where applicable.

How it's handled

Data associated with your account is handled according to our deletion process and Privacy Policy.

If you signed in with Google, deleting your CapLink account does not delete your Google account.

What CapLink does not require

  • CapLink does not connect directly to your bank account.
  • CapLink does not automatically retrieve your bank transactions.
  • CapLink does not ask for your bank password, UPI PIN, debit/credit card PIN, CVV, or internet banking credentials.
  • Financial information used by CapLink is generally information that you choose to provide inside the application.
