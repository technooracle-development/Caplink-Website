Delete Your CapLink Account

You can delete your CapLink account at any time, directly from within the CapLink app.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open CapLinkSign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to ProfileTap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation.
  3. Open Settings / AccountFind your account settings.
  4. Select Delete AccountRead the on-screen information about what will be removed.
  5. Confirm deletionConfirm to complete the request.

Menu names may vary slightly between app versions. If a step looks different, look for "Delete Account" in your profile or account settings.

What happens when you delete your account

  • Your CapLink account is closed and you'll be signed out.
  • Data associated with your account — such as profile details and the financial information you entered — is deleted according to CapLink's account deletion process.
  • Some information may be retained for a limited period where required by law, to resolve disputes, prevent misuse, or as part of routine backups until they are overwritten.
  • Deletion can't be undone. If you use CapLink again later, you'll need to create a new account.

If you signed in with Google, deleting your CapLink account does not delete your Google account. You can also review app access from your Google account settings.

Can't access the delete option?

If you have trouble using the in-app deletion feature — for example, you can't sign in — email us from the address linked to your CapLink account and we'll help.

technooracleinfo@gmail.com

For your security, we may need to confirm that you own the account before processing a request. We'll never ask for your password.
