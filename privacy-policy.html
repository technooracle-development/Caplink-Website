Summary

The short version: CapLink does not connect to your bank accounts or read your bank transactions. You create an account with email/password or Google Sign-In, and you choose what financial information to enter. We use that information to run calculations, comparisons, Financial Health insights, and AI responses for you. You can delete your account from within the app — see Delete Your Account.

This summary is for convenience. Please read the full policy below.

1. Who we are

CapLink ("CapLink", "we", "us", or "our") is a financial education, calculation, comparison, and decision-support mobile application published by TechnoOracle. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the CapLink mobile app and this website (together, the "Services").

CapLink is not a bank, broker, lender, insurance provider, tax authority, or investment adviser.

2. Information you provide

Depending on how you use CapLink, you may provide:

  • Account information — your email address and the authentication information needed to create and secure your account.
  • Google account information — if you choose Google Sign-In, basic profile information that Google shares with us with your permission, such as your name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Financial information you enter manually — for example income, expenses, EMIs, debt, investments, emergency savings, insurance details, and purchase-related details (see Financial information).
  • Personal context — such as your age, number of dependents, and financial goals, when you choose to provide them.
  • Calculator and comparison inputs — the values you enter into calculators and decision tools, and any calculations you save.
  • Messages to CapLink Assistant — the questions you ask and related context (see AI features).
  • Communications with us — the content of emails or messages you send us, such as support requests or feedback.

3. Information collected automatically

Where applicable, we and our service providers may automatically collect limited technical and usage information needed to operate, secure, and improve the Services, such as:

  • App usage and activity information, such as features used and actions taken in the app;
  • Device and technical information, such as device type, operating system, app version, and diagnostic or error logs;
  • Standard request information, such as IP address and timestamps, processed when the app communicates with our servers or when you visit this website.

4. What CapLink does not collect through bank connections

CapLink does not connect directly to your bank accounts and does not automatically retrieve your bank transactions or account statements. Any financial figures in CapLink are values you type in yourself.

CapLink does not ask for, and you should never enter into CapLink:

  • Bank or internet banking passwords or login credentials;
  • UPI PINs;
  • Debit card PINs;
  • Credit or debit card numbers or CVV codes;
  • One-time passwords (OTPs) for accessing bank accounts or authorising payments.

If anyone claiming to represent CapLink asks for this information, please do not share it and let us know at technooracleinfo@gmail.com.

5. Authentication

You can create and access a CapLink account using:

  • Email and password — your email address is used to identify your account, and your password is handled by our authentication provider to verify sign-ins.
  • Google Sign-In — Google authenticates you and shares basic profile information with us according to the permissions you approve. We do not receive your Google password.

As with any sign-in system, authentication involves processing account identifiers and security tokens or session data so that you can stay signed in securely.

6. Financial information

To use certain features, you may choose to manually enter information such as:

  • Income (for example, salary or monthly income) and monthly expenses;
  • EMIs and other debt;
  • Investments and emergency fund savings;
  • Financial goals;
  • Age and number of dependents;
  • Insurance information;
  • Purchase information, such as the price of a car, home, bike, or gadget you're considering.

We use this information to calculate results, compare financial options, generate Financial Health insights, and personalise educational guidance for you. Providing it is optional, but some features won't work without the relevant inputs.

7. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage your account;
  • Provide calculators, comparisons, affordability checks, and Financial Health scores;
  • Generate AI responses and personalised educational insights;
  • Save calculations and preferences you choose to keep;
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Services;
  • Respond to your questions, feedback, and requests;
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use.

8. CapLink Assistant and AI features

When you use CapLink Assistant or AI Insight features, the content of your request — and relevant information you've provided in CapLink, such as Financial Health inputs or goals — may be processed to generate a response. This processing may involve third-party AI service providers acting on our behalf.

  • AI-generated responses are produced automatically and can be inaccurate, incomplete, or out of date. They are for educational and awareness purposes only and are not professional financial, investment, tax, legal, or insurance advice.
  • Please share only what's needed for your question. Avoid entering highly sensitive information that CapLink does not require, such as identity document numbers, account numbers, passwords, or health details.

9. Infrastructure and storage

CapLink uses Supabase infrastructure for authentication and database services. Information you provide in the app is stored using this infrastructure.

We use trusted infrastructure and reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect user information. Your information may be stored or processed in countries other than the one you live in, depending on where our service providers operate.

10. Sharing and service providers

We share information only as needed to run CapLink and as described in this policy. Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate the app — for example authentication, database, hosting, analytics, AI, or other infrastructure providers, where applicable. These providers process information on our behalf to deliver their services.

We may also disclose information if required by law or legal process, to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users, CapLink, or others, or in connection with a business transfer such as a merger or acquisition, subject to appropriate safeguards.

11. Data retention

We keep information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services. We may retain certain information for longer where necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent misuse, or enforce our agreements.

12. Account Deletion

You can delete your CapLink account from within the application. If you cannot access your account or the deletion option, you can contact us at technooracleinfo@gmail.com to request assistance with account deletion.

When you delete your account, data associated with your account is handled according to the app's deletion process and applicable legal, security, and operational requirements. Some information may be retained for a limited period where required — for example in backups or logs until they are overwritten, or where retention is legally required.

13. Security

We use industry-standard security practices and trusted infrastructure to help protect your information, such as encrypted (HTTPS) connections between the app and our backend services. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your login details private and let us know if you suspect unauthorised access to your account.

14. Your choices

  • Choose what to enter. Financial details are optional and entered by you. You can leave out anything you're not comfortable sharing.
  • Edit your information. You can update inputs, such as your Financial Health answers, within the app.
  • Delete your account. You can delete your account from within the app at any time. See Delete Your Account.
  • Contact us. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to withdraw consent. To make a request, email technooracleinfo@gmail.com.

15. Children's privacy

CapLink is intended for adults who manage their own finances and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example to reflect changes to the Services or legal requirements. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continuing to use CapLink after an update means the updated policy applies.

17. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact TechnoOracle at technooracleinfo@gmail.com.
