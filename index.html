What CapLink does

One place for smarter money decisions.

Big financial choices are easier when you can see the numbers side by side. CapLink brings the tools together in one calm, simple app.

Compare

Put two options side by side — rent or buy, cash or loan — and see the trade-offs over time.

Calculate

SIP, EMI, FD, gold, tax, retirement and more — quick estimates from the numbers you enter.

Plan

Check affordability before a big purchase and map out goals like retirement or an emergency fund.

Understand

Plain-language explanations and AI-powered insights help you make sense of every result.

Privacy & trust

Built with privacy in mind.

CapLink works from the information you choose to type in. There's no bank linking and nothing is pulled from your accounts.

  • No bank account connectionCapLink never links to your bank.
  • No bank transaction accessNothing is read from your statements.
  • No OTP collectionCapLink doesn't ask for banking OTPs.
  • No card details requiredNo card numbers, CVV, or UPI PIN.
  • Google Sign-In availableSign in with your Google account.
  • Email authentication availableOr use email and password.
  • You control what you shareEnter only the numbers you choose.
  • Account deletion availableDelete it in the app. How to delete
  • Supabase backendTrusted infrastructure for auth and data.

We use industry-standard security practices and trusted infrastructure to help protect your information. Read our Privacy Policy.
