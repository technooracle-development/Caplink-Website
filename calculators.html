Calculators

Financial calculators, made clear.

Ten focused calculators for investing, loans, savings, tax, gold, and retirement. Enter your numbers, adjust the assumptions, and see what changes.

Browse calculators Get CapLink

Calculator directory

Filter by category, then tap Explore to see what each calculator does. The calculators themselves run inside the CapLink app.

Investing

SIP

Estimate how regular monthly investments could grow over time.

Explore

You enter: monthly amount, duration, expected annual return.

You see: total invested, estimated gains, and estimated final value.

Returns aren't guaranteed; try a range of rates to see different outcomes.

Investing

Lumpsum

See how a one-time investment might grow over a chosen period.

Explore

You enter: amount invested, duration, expected annual return.

You see: estimated value at the end of the period and estimated gains.

An estimate based on a constant rate; real markets move up and down.

Investing

SWP

Plan regular withdrawals from an investment and see how long it could last.

Explore

You enter: starting corpus, monthly withdrawal, expected return, duration.

You see: total withdrawn and estimated balance remaining.

Useful for thinking about income in retirement. Results are illustrative.

Loans

EMI

Work out a monthly instalment for a home, car, or personal loan.

Explore

You enter: loan amount, interest rate, tenure.

You see: monthly EMI, total interest, and total amount payable.

Your lender's actual EMI may differ due to fees, rate type, or rounding.

Loans

Loan

Understand the full cost of borrowing and how tenure changes it.

Explore

You enter: principal, interest rate, tenure.

You see: how much goes to interest versus principal over the loan's life.

Compare tenures to see the trade-off between EMI size and total interest.

Savings

FD

Estimate the maturity value of a fixed deposit.

Explore

You enter: deposit amount, interest rate, tenure, compounding frequency.

You see: estimated interest earned and maturity amount.

Bank rates, tax, and premature-withdrawal rules can change the actual amount.

Gold

Gold

Check gold value by purity and weight and view price trends.

Explore

You choose: purity (24K, 22K, 18K) and weight.

You see: an indicative value and a historical price trend.

Indicative prices only. Jeweller and dealer prices include making charges and taxes. Past trends don't predict future prices.

Retirement

Retirement

Estimate how much you may need for retirement and how to work toward it.

Explore

You enter: current age, retirement age, monthly expenses, savings, assumptions.

You see: an estimated corpus and the monthly saving that may help reach it.

Long-term projections are sensitive to assumptions; review them regularly.

General Finance

Inflation

See how rising prices could affect what your money buys in future.

Explore

You enter: today's cost, inflation rate, number of years.

You see: the estimated future cost and the change in purchasing power.

Actual inflation varies year to year and by category of spending.

Taxes

Tax

Get an estimate of income tax based on the income and deductions you enter.

Explore

You enter: income details and applicable deductions.

You see: an estimated tax amount to help you plan.

For awareness only — not tax filing or tax advice. Tax rules change; confirm with official sources or a tax professional.
