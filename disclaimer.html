Financial Disclaimer

Please read this before relying on anything you see in CapLink.

Last updated: October 2026

In short: CapLink helps you learn, calculate, and compare. It does not provide guaranteed financial advice. Results are estimates, AI responses can be wrong, and important decisions deserve independent verification.

What CapLink is

CapLink is a financial education, calculation, comparison, and decision-support application published by TechnoOracle. It is designed to help you understand financial concepts and see the trade-offs between options.

CapLink is not a bank, broker, investment adviser, financial institution, tax authority, insurance provider, or lender, and it does not provide guaranteed financial advice.

Calculator outputs are estimates

Calculator, comparison, affordability, and Financial Health results are estimates based on the information you enter and on assumptions such as interest rates, expected returns, inflation, and prices. They are illustrations, not predictions or promises.

  • Actual outcomes can differ from what CapLink shows.
  • Investment returns are not guaranteed, and the value of investments can go down as well as up.
  • Past performance — such as historical gold price trends — does not indicate future results.
  • Interest rates, prices, tax rules, and product terms change over time and vary by provider.
  • Indicative prices or rates shown in the app may be delayed or differ from the price you're offered.

The Financial Health score

The Financial Health score is an educational planning tool based on information you provide. It is not a credit score, investment rating, or professional financial assessment.

AI responses

CapLink Assistant and AI Insight responses are generated automatically and may be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect. They do not account for every detail of your situation and should not be treated as personalised professional financial, investment, tax, legal, or insurance advice.

Verify important decisions

Before making important financial, tax, investment, insurance, loan, or legal decisions, please verify information independently and, where appropriate, consult a suitably qualified professional — such as a registered financial adviser, chartered accountant, tax professional, insurance adviser, or lawyer.

No guarantees

CapLink does not guarantee savings, profits, investment returns, loan approvals, or financial success. Any decision you make is your own, and you are responsible for it.
